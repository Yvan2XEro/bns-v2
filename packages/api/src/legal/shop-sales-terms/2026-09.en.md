# General sales terms

## Parties and seller of record

The sale is concluded between the buyer and the shop identified in the order
summary, referred to below as "the seller". BuyNSellem acts only as a
hosting and matchmaking platform (art. 30): it is not a party to the sale or
to the delivery, and does not act as the seller.

## Object of the contract

The contract covers the items listed in the order summary, with their
essential characteristics (title, variant, condition, displayed attributes),
at the quantities and prices shown when the order was placed.

## Price and payment on delivery

The price shown for each item is all taxes included. The delivery fee is
shown separately and is added to the items' total to form the total amount
due. Payment is made in cash, only at the moment the parcel is handed over.

## Delivery and ETA

The seller delivers within the area and timeframe shown in the order
summary.

## Seller's acceptance

The seller has 48 hours from the moment the order is placed to accept it.
Otherwise, the order is automatically cancelled.

## Buyer's withdrawal right (art. 20)

The buyer has 15 days from receipt of the parcel to exercise their
withdrawal right, without justification, using the "Return an item" button
on the order. Return costs are borne by the buyer, except when the item is
defective or not as described (annex A6).

The withdrawal right does not apply to customised goods, perishable goods,
or goods unsealed by the buyer that cannot be returned for hygiene or health
protection reasons.

## Conformity and legal guarantees

The seller remains bound by the legal conformity guarantees that apply to
the sale, whichever platform was used to conclude it.

## Void clause (law 2011/012, art. 5)

Any clause of this contract that would have the effect of limiting the
seller's legal liability is void and of no effect.

## Complaints

A complaint is first addressed to the seller through the order's
conversation. If it goes unanswered or unresolved, the buyer may contact
BuyNSellem support.

## Applicable law

This contract is governed by Cameroonian law, in particular law 2011/012 on
electronic commerce.
