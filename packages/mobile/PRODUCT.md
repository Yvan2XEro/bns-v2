# Buy'N'Sellem — Product

## What it is
A Cameroonian marketplace. Individuals post classifieds; verified shops run a catalogue, take orders, ship through couriers and riders, and get paid through mobile money (NotchPay: MTN MoMo, Orange Money). Buyers browse, chat with sellers, call them, add to cart, check out, track a delivery, open a return or a dispute.

## Who uses it, and where
- Buyers in Douala, Yaoundé and secondary cities, mostly on mid-range Android phones, often outdoors or in a moving taxi, under strong daylight, on metered data.
- Sellers: individuals clearing things out, and small shops that live on the app all day (orders, inbox, payouts).
- Couriers / riders on a bike, one hand free.
- Moderators working through queues.

Bilingual French / English, French first. Prices in FCFA.

## What success looks like
- A buyer judges a listing (photo, price, place, seller trust) in one glance from the feed.
- A seller posts a listing in under two minutes.
- Trust is visible: verified shops, response speed, reviews, protected payment.

## Constraints
- Platform-native controls, navigation and gestures on iOS and Android. No web costumes.
- Legible in sunlight: high contrast, large prices, no thin grey text.
- Light and dark themes, user-selectable.
- Performance first: FlashList feeds, cheap shadows, no heavy blur.

## Brand commitments
- Name: Buy'N'Sellem. The apostrophes are part of the mark.
- Blue + yellow heritage from the web, kept as indigo + signage yellow on mobile.
